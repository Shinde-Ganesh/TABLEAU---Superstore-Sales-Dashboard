# TABLEAU---Superstore-Sales-Dashboard

Tableau Dashboard from Raw Data for Superstore Sales

**Project Overview**

The Superstore Sales Dashboard 2023 is an interactive Tableau analytical tool designed to track, compare, and visualize key retail performance metrics against the previous year (2022). This dashboard empowers stakeholders to monitor top-line revenue, profitability volatility, and quantity sold while drilling down into category-level performance and temporal trends.

**Dashboard Visualizations & Structure**

1. Top-Level KPI Trend Cards

  * Total Sales Trend: Displays monthly revenue trajectory comparing 2023 (black line) with 2022 (grey baseline), highlighting January as the lowest month and December as the highest month.

  * Total Profit Trend: Tracks monthly profitability swings, highlighting profit spikes and dips relative to the prior year.

  * Total Quantity Trend: Monitors unit volume movement across the year through November/December peak periods.

2. Subcategory Comparison (Sales & Profit by Subcategory)

  * Subcategory Bar Comparison: Ranks subcategories by sales volume (Phones, Chairs, Binders, Storage, Copiers, Tables, Accessories, Machines, Appliances, Bookcases, Furnishings, Paper, Supplies, Art, Labels, Envelopes, Fasteners).

  * Profit vs. Loss Breakdown: Side-by-side divergent bar chart identifying profitable subcategories (blue) versus loss-making subcategories (orange/red markers), notably highlighting losses in Tables and select lower-tier items.

3. Sales & Profit Trends over Time

  * Temporal Breakdown Charts: Dual line/bar charts tracking performance against benchmark averages (AVG Sales $14K, AVG Profit $2K), color-coded to distinguish performance periods above (blue) and below (orange) the baseline.


**Key Business Insights**

  * Revenue Growth: Total sales achieved a solid 20.36% year-over-year increase, driven by strong sustained order volumes in the second half of the year.

  * Profitability Divergence: While top-line sales are robust at $733K, profit margins experience severe volatility, with certain subcategories like Tables operating at a net loss despite high sales rankings.

  * Seasonality & Spikes: Sales velocity peaks noticeably toward Q4 (ending in December), whereas profit trends experience sharp anomalous spikes and troughs requiring deeper discount policy reviews.


**Tools & Techniques Used**

  * Tableau Desktop / Public: Dashboard layout design, KPI card creation, dual-axis trend analysis, and dynamic filtering actions.

  * Data Visualizations: Divergent bar charts, reference lines for averages, custom color palettes (Profit vs. Loss), and comparative year-over-year indicators.
